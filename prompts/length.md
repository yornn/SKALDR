# Length target

- The draft as handed to you: about {{skaldr_draft_words}} words.
- The finished post: **{{skaldr_target}} words**, and it must land between {{skaldr_min}} and {{skaldr_max}}.
- Shape it into roughly {{skaldr_paragraphs}} paragraph(s).

Compare those two numbers and work accordingly.

| The draft against the target | What the post needs |
|---|---|
| Far under — the target is twice the draft or more | The draft is a seed: it tells you *what happens*, and almost nothing else. Write the moment out in full — the body, the senses, the thinking between the actions, the beats around every spoken line. The same events, with the whole scene around them instead of a summary of them. |
| Somewhat under | Rewrite it and open it up. Give each action its own space, let the details the draft already names actually land, put air around the speech. |
| About the same | Rewrite in place. Sharpen the prose without stretching the post or trimming it. |
| Over the target | Tighten. Repetition, filler and doubled description go first. Every fact, every spoken line and the ending survive the cut — only the wording goes. |

The count is a target, not a quota. Never pad to reach it: no restating a sentence in different words, no piling up detail for its own sake, no drifting into the next scene, and nothing from the "you may not invent" list. If the honest version of this post lands a little under {{skaldr_min}}, hand back the honest version.
